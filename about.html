Our Heritage

A Legacy of
Timeless Elegance

Founded in 2011, Mon Étoile emerged from a profound belief: that true luxury transcends gender. Our founder, Alexandre Moreau, envisioned a world where accessories celebrated individuality rather than conformity — where a watch, a necklace, or a leather bag spoke to the person wearing it, not the gender society assigned them.

From our atelier in Paris to boutiques spanning four continents, every Mon Étoile piece is a testament to this vision. We partner with master craftsmen across Switzerland, Italy, and Japan to create accessories that honor tradition while embracing modernity.

Our commitment to excellence extends beyond aesthetics. We source ethically mined precious metals, use sustainably tanned leathers, and ensure fair wages for every artisan who touches a Mon Étoile creation. Because true luxury must be responsible.

The Process

From Vision
to Reality

Every Mon Étoile piece begins as a sketch in our Paris atelier. Our design team draws inspiration from architecture, nature, and the diverse individuals who wear our creations. Each design undergoes months of refinement before production begins.

Our timepieces are assembled in Switzerland, where generations of watchmakers apply their expertise to every movement. Our jewelry is hand-finished in Florence, where goldsmiths employ techniques passed down through centuries. Our leather goods are crafted in Tuscany, using vegetable-tanned hides that develop a unique patina over time.

The result is accessories that are not merely beautiful, but meaningful — pieces that become part of your story.
